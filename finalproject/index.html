Welcome to Your New Morning Stop

Located in the heart of Mishawaka, we believe that every great day starts with two things: perfectly browned toast and a latte made with love.

Our shop at 64789 Rising Sun Road is a community hub where the smell of fresh sourdough meets the buzz of our custom espresso machines.

Our Story

HyperToast started in a tiny kitchen with a loud toaster and a louder dream. We named it after our founder's habit of getting 'hyper' every time the morning toast smelled just right. Today, we bring that same energy to every latte we stir and every loaf we bake.
